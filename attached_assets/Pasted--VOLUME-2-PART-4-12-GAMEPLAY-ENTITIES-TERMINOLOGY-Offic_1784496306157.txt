------------------------------------------------------------
OFFICIAL GAME ENTITIES
------------------------------------------------------------

Series

A complete match consisting of multiple Rounds.

Series ends only when:

• A Team reaches +52 or higher.

OR

• Bidder completes a Perfect 8/8 Bid.

------------------------------------------------------------

Round

One complete deal of cards.

Every Round contains:

• Card Distribution

• Bidding

• Trump Selection

• 8 Tricks

• Round Result

• Score Update

------------------------------------------------------------

Trick

One complete cycle where every active player plays exactly one legal card.

After every player has played:

• Trick Winner is determined.

• Cards are collected.

• Winner leads the next Trick.

------------------------------------------------------------

Card

A playing card owned by exactly one player.

A card can exist in only one zone at a time.

------------------------------------------------------------

A permanent group of players.

OFFICIAL GAMEPLAY TERMS
------------------------------------------------------------

Dealer

Player authorized to distribute cards.

------------------------------------------------------------

Bid

Target number of Tricks declared by Bidder.

Allowed values:

5

6

7

8

------------------------------------------------------------

Primary Bid

Mandatory opening Bid.

Always:

5

------------------------------------------------------------

Primary Trump

Trump selected during the Primary Bid.

------------------------------------------------------------

Final Bid

Highest Bid after two bidding rounds.

------------------------------------------------------------

Final Trump

Official Trump Suit used for the entire Round.

------------------------------------------------------------

Lead

First card played in a Trick.

------------------------------------------------------------

Lead Suit

Suit of the Lead Card.

------------------------------------------------------------

Trump

Official winning Suit selected after bidding.

Trump is optional.

Trump is never mandatory.

------------------------------------------------------------

Trick Winner

Player who legally wins the current Trick according to the Rulebook.

------------------------------------------------------------

Round Winner

Team that successfully satisfies Rulebook Round Winning conditions.

Not necessarily the Team with more Tricks.

------------------------------------------------------------

Series Winner

Team that wins the complete Series.
