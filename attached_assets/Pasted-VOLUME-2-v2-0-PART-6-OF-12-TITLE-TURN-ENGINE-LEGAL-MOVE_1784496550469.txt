TITLE:
TURN ENGINE & LEGAL MOVE VALIDATION

READ ONLY.

Do not compare with repository.

Do not perform conflict analysis.

Do not suggest implementation.

Only memorize the official gameplay specification below.

------------------------------------------------------------

OFFICIAL TURN ENGINE

The Server always owns the turn.

Only one player may have the turn at any time.

No simultaneous turns are ever allowed.

A player's turn begins only after the previous legal move has been completely validated and committed by the server.

If a player disconnects during his turn:

• Wait for the configured reconnect timeout.
• If the player reconnects, continue the same turn.
• Otherwise AI immediately completes that turn using only legal moves.

------------------------------------------------------------

TURN ORDER

The order of play is always clockwise.

After every legal card:

Server immediately advances the turn to the next active seat.

No player may skip another player.

No player may play twice in the same Trick.

Every player must play exactly one card per Trick.

After every player has played exactly one card:

The Trick becomes complete.

------------------------------------------------------------

LEGAL MOVE VALIDATION

Before accepting any card, the Server must validate:

1. Is this the player's turn?

2. Does the player still own this card?

3. Has this card already been played?

4. Is the selected card currently playable?

5. Is Lead Suit available inside:
   • Face-up Cards
   • Secret Hand Cards

If YES:

Only Lead Suit cards are legal.

Face-down cannot be opened.

Trump cannot be played.

Other suits are illegal.

If NO:

Player may legally play:

• Any Face-up card

• Any Secret Hand card

• OR open exactly one Face-down card.

------------------------------------------------------------

FACE-DOWN VALIDATION

Opening Face-down is legal ONLY when:

Lead Suit does not exist in:

• Face-up zone

AND

• Secret Hand zone.

Player may choose ANY Face-down position.

Server reveals only that card.

That revealed card becomes compulsory.

No replacement.

No second selection.

No cancellation.

------------------------------------------------------------

TRUMP VALIDATION

Trump is NEVER mandatory.

Overcut NEVER exists.

If Lead Suit is unavailable:

Player may:

Play Trump,

Play another Suit,

Play Secret,

OR reveal Face-down.

All are legal.

------------------------------------------------------------

ILLEGAL MOVE

If any illegal move is attempted:

Server rejects it.

Original card ownership remains unchanged.

Penalty rules defined in Rule-20 will apply.

Turn does NOT advance until a legal move is accepted.

------------------------------------------------------------

TURN COMPLETION

Immediately after a legal card:

Server updates:

• Played card
• Card ownership
• Player zones
• Trick state
• Public visibility

Then turn advances clockwise.

------------------------------------------------------------

AUTHORITATIVE PRINCIPLE

Clients never decide legality.

Clients never decide Trick winner.

Clients never decide turn ownership.

Server validates everything.

This rule is immutable.
