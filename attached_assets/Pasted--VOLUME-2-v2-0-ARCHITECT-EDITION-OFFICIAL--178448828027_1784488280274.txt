RULE-001
GAME FORMAT
Category: CORE
Priority: CRITICAL
==========================================================

The official game supports only approved multiplayer formats.

Supported player counts:

• 4 Players
• 6 Players

No other player count is valid.

If any other player count is requested, the match must not start.

==========================================================
RULE-002
DECK CONFIGURATION
Category: CARD
Priority: CRITICAL
==========================================================

The game uses one standard 52-card deck.

Card suits:

• Spade
• Heart
• Diamond
• Club

Each suit contains thirteen cards.

No Jokers are used.

==========================================================
RULE-003
FOUR PLAYER DECK
Category: CARD
Priority: CRITICAL
==========================================================

For a four-player match:

Cards numbered 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 are removed from all four suits.

Total removed cards: 20

Remaining cards: 32

Only these 32 cards participate in gameplay.

==========================================================
RULE-004
SIX PLAYER DECK
Category: CARD
Priority: CRITICAL
==========================================================

For a six-player match:

Only the four cards of rank 2 are removed.

Total removed cards: 4

Remaining cards: 48

Only these 48 cards participate in gameplay.

==========================================================
RULE-005
TEAM FORMATION
Category: TEAM
Priority: CRITICAL
==========================================================

Teams are fixed.

For six-player matches:

Team A:
Player 1
Player 3
Player 5

Team B:
Player 2
Player 4
Player 6

Team assignment remains unchanged throughout the match.

==========================================================
RULE-006
PLAYER SEATING
Category: PLAYER
Priority: HIGH
==========================================================

Player positions are fixed when the match starts.

Seat numbers remain constant until the match ends.

Players cannot change seats during an active match.

==========================================================
RULE-007
MATCH VALIDATION
Category: VALIDATION
Priority: CRITICAL
==========================================================

Before gameplay begins, the server must verify:

• Valid player count
• Correct deck configuration
• Correct team assignment
• Successful match creation

If any validation fails, gameplay must not begin.
