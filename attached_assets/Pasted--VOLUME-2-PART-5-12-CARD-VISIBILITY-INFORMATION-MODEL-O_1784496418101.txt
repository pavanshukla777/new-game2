# VOLUME 2 – PART 5/12
## CARD VISIBILITY & INFORMATION MODEL
### Official Chhakri Rulebook Specification
### Based ONLY on RULE-001 to RULE-030

------------------------------------------------------------
OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------

This document defines what information is visible to each participant during a Chhakri match.

Information visibility is a core gameplay rule.

No player may receive information beyond what is explicitly allowed by the Rulebook.

------------------------------------------------------------
INFORMATION PRINCIPLE
------------------------------------------------------------

Every card always exists in exactly one visibility state.

Visibility is controlled only by the Server.

Clients never decide card visibility.

------------------------------------------------------------
PLAYER CARD ZONES
------------------------------------------------------------

Each player owns exactly:

• 2 Secret Hand Cards
• 3 Face-down Cards
• 3 Face-up Cards

Total:

8 Cards

------------------------------------------------------------
SECRET HAND CARDS
------------------------------------------------------------

Quantity:

2

Visible To:

• Owner only

Hidden From:

• Opponent Team
• Partner
• Spectators
• Every other player

Server always knows these cards.

------------------------------------------------------------
FACE-DOWN CARDS
------------------------------------------------------------

Quantity:

3

Visible To:

No player.

Hidden From:

• Owner
• Partner
• Opponents
• Spectators

These cards remain hidden until legally opened according to the Rulebook.

Only the opened card becomes visible.

Remaining Face-down cards stay hidden.

------------------------------------------------------------
FACE-UP CARDS
------------------------------------------------------------

Quantity:

3

Visible To:

• Every player
• Both Teams
• Spectators

These cards remain public information.

Whenever a Face-up card is played, it disappears from that player's Face-up zone.

No replacement card is added.

------------------------------------------------------------
PLAYED CARDS
------------------------------------------------------------

Once a card is legally played:

• Every player can see it.
• Spectators can see it.
• Server records it.
• The card cannot return to any player's card zone.

------------------------------------------------------------
TRICK COLLECTION
------------------------------------------------------------

After a Trick is completed:

All played cards move to the Winner's Trick Collection.

Cards are no longer playable.

They remain part of the completed Trick history.

------------------------------------------------------------
SERVER VISIBILITY
------------------------------------------------------------

The Server always knows:

• Complete Deck
• Card Ownership
• Secret Hand Cards
• Face-down Cards
• Face-up Cards
• Played Cards
• Trick Collections

The Server is the only complete source of truth.

------------------------------------------------------------
PLAYER KNOWLEDGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Players may know only:

• Their own Secret Hand Cards
• All Face-up Cards
• Cards already played
• Public Bid information
• Public Trump information
• Public Score information

Nothing else.

------------------------------------------------------------
SPECTATOR KNOWLEDGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Spectators follow the same visibility rules defined by the Rulebook.

No hidden gameplay information may be revealed unless a future Rulebook version explicitly allows it.

------------------------------------------------------------
VISIBILITY LOCK
------------------------------------------------------------

No gameplay action may accidentally reveal:

• Secret Hand Cards
• Face-down Cards

Information leakage is prohibited.

------------------------------------------------------------
ZERO ASSUMPTION POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

Visibility rules are fixed.

No Client, AI, Spectator or future feature may bypass these rules unless the Rulebook is officially updated.
