------------------------------------------------------------

OFFICIAL TRICK RESOLUTION

A Trick becomes complete only after every active player has played exactly one legal card.

6 Players:
Exactly 6 cards.

4 Players:
Exactly 4 cards.

No Trick may end early.

------------------------------------------------------------

TRICK WINNER

If no Trump exists:

Highest card of Lead Suit wins.

If one or more Trump cards exist:

Highest Trump wins.

Lead Suit cannot defeat any Trump.

Overcut is never mandatory.

Trump is always optional.

------------------------------------------------------------

POST TRICK PROCESS

Immediately after Trick Winner is determined:

Server shall:

• Lock Trick Result
• Store played cards
• Increase Winner Team Trick Count
• Increase Winner Player Trick Count
• Move all Trick cards to Winner Collection
• Remove cards from active play
• Update Live Progress Panel
• Make Winner the next Lead Player

Only after all updates are committed:

Next Trick begins.

------------------------------------------------------------

ROUND COMPLETION

One Round always contains exactly:

8 Tricks.

Round ends only after Trick 8 finishes.

No early Round completion.

------------------------------------------------------------

TARGET VALIDATION

Bidder Target:

Equals Final Bid.

Examples:

Bid 5 → Target 5

Bid 6 → Target 6

Bid 7 → Target 7

Bid 8 → Target 8

Opponent Target:

Bid 5 → 4

Bid 6 → 3

Bid 7 → 2

Bid 8 → 1

------------------------------------------------------------

ROUND WINNER

Round Winner is determined ONLY by target completion.

Never by total tricks alone.

Example:

Bid Team = 5 tricks after bidding 6.

Opponent = 3 tricks.

Although 5 > 3,

Bid Team failed.

Opponent reached target.

Opponent wins Round.

------------------------------------------------------------

SCORING

If Bid Success:

Bid Team +Bid

Opponent -Bid

If Bid Failure:

Bid Team -(2×Bid)

Opponent +(2×Bid)

Score always remains Zero Sum.

------------------------------------------------------------

SERIES ENGINE

Series ends immediately when:

Either Team reaches +52.

OR

A Bid Team successfully completes an 8 Bid by winning all 8 Tricks.

This is an Instant Series Victory.

Series immediately ends.

Fresh Series begins.

------------------------------------------------------------

NEXT ROUND

If Series continues:

Team currently behind in Match Score becomes Dealer Team.

Distribution order never changes:

------------------------------------------------------------

SERVER AUTHORITY

Only Server may:

Declare Trick Winner.

Declare Round Winner.

Update Score.

Declare Series Winner.

Start Next Round.

Start New Series.

These decisions are immutable.
